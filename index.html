FLIP SHOOTER

One plane, two points of view. Flip the camera mid-flight to slip over walls and around pylons.

Coming soon to the App Store Support
Free · iPhone, iPad and iPhone Duo

How it plays

Flip the view

Fly from above to steer around pylons, then flip to the side to climb over walls or dive under them.

Faster every stage

The longer you last, the faster it gets, with tighter gaps and more enemies. Five hearts. One best score.

Climb the leaderboard

Every run is posted to Game Center. See where you rank against friends and every other pilot.

Drag to steer

Drag anywhere to fly. Your plane fires on its own, so you can focus on flying.

Bomb when it counts

Charge up a bomb and clear every enemy on screen when things get tight.

No account needed

No sign-in, no setup. Open it and fly. Sound can be switched off any time.

Fold it like a laptop

On iPhone Duo, half-fold it and stand it on the table. The game plays on the top half, and the bottom half becomes a trackpad, with BOMB, FLIP and Sound right in front of your thumbs.
