How to play

  • Steer: drag anywhere on the screen. Your plane fires on its own.
  • FLIP: swings the camera between the top view and the side view.
  • Walls: flip to the side view and fly over or under them.
  • Pylons: flip to the top view and steer around them.
  • BOMB: when the bar under your hearts is full, tap BOMB to clear every enemy on screen.

The longer you survive, the faster it gets. You have five hearts per run.

Common questions

How do I turn the sound off?

Tap the speaker button in the top-left corner during a run, or the Sound button on the title and game-over screens. The game remembers your choice. It also stays quiet when your iPhone's silent switch is on.

How does reviving work?

When you're shot down you can tap REVIVE once per run. Watch a short video ad and you carry on with your score kept. If no ad is available at that moment, the button doesn't appear.

I bought Remove Ads. How do I get it back on a new device?

Sign in with the same Apple Account, open Flip Shooter and tap Restore purchase on the title screen. With Remove Ads, reviving is free and no ads are shown.

Why isn't my score on the leaderboard?

Scores are posted through Apple's Game Center, so you need to be signed in to Game Center in your device's Settings. Once you are, each finished run is sent and your best score is kept.

How do I change my leaderboard name?

The leaderboard shows your Game Center nickname. You can change it in Settings under Game Center.

What is laptop mode on iPhone Duo?

Half-fold your iPhone Duo and stand it on its side like a small laptop. The game plays on the top half, and the bottom half becomes a trackpad: drag on it to steer, with BOMB, FLIP and Sound in a row just under the fold.

Does Flip Shooter collect my data?

Flip Shooter itself doesn't collect anything, and there's no account to create. The video ads are provided by Google AdMob. See the privacy policy for details.
