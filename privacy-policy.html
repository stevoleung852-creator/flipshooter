Flip Shooter Privacy Policy

Effective 22 November 2026

Flip Shooter is a game for iPhone and iPad made by Stephen Leung ("I", "me"). This page explains what information the game handles and why.

The short version: the game has no accounts and I do not collect or store any information about you. The game shows optional video ads through Google AdMob, and Google collects some device information to deliver them.

Information I collect

None. Flip Shooter has no sign-in, no account and no server of its own. I never receive your name, email address, contacts, photos or precise location.

Information stored on your device

The game saves two things on your device so they are there next time you play:

  • your best score, and
  • whether you have bought Remove Ads.

This stays on your device. Deleting the game deletes it.

Advertising

When you are shot down, the game offers a REVIVE button. If you choose it, a video ad is shown by Google AdMob, an advertising service run by Google. Ads are never shown unless you tap that button.

To load and show ads, and to measure them and prevent fraud, Google's software inside the game may collect:

  • your IP address, which can indicate your general location such as city or country,
  • device identifiers and device details such as model and system version,
  • how you interact with ads, such as whether an ad was shown, finished or tapped, and
  • diagnostic information such as crash and performance data.

The game does not ask for permission to track you across other companies' apps and websites, so your device's advertising identifier is not shared with Google by this game.

Google handles this information under its own policies. See Google's Privacy Policy and how Google uses information from apps that use its services.

If you buy Remove Ads, the game no longer shows ads, and from the next time it is opened it does not start Google's advertising software at all.

Purchases

The Remove Ads purchase is handled entirely by Apple through the App Store. I never see your payment details. Apple tells the game only whether the purchase has been made.

Leaderboard

If you are signed in to Apple's Game Center, the game sends your score to the Flip Shooter leaderboard, where it appears next to your Game Center name. Game Center is run by Apple under Apple's Privacy Policy. If you are not signed in to Game Center, no score is sent. I do not receive or store your Game Center details myself.

Children

Flip Shooter is not directed at children under 13, and I do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Your choices

  • You can play without ever watching an ad; reviving is optional.
  • You can buy Remove Ads so that no ads are shown.
  • You can delete the game at any time, which removes everything it saved on your device.
  • You can manage ad personalisation for your Apple devices in Settings, under Privacy & Security.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
